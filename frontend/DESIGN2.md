## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a high-resolution, institutional-grade geospatial and carbon intelligence aesthetic. The brand communicates unyielding scientific rigor, geospatial precision, and architectural balance. Designed for climate-tech analysts, institutional ESG asset allocators, and carbon registry certifiers, the interface must project transparency, verifiable truth, and clinical calm.

The visual style is **Minimalist Architectural High-Tech**:
- Zero-radius precision geometry with uncompromising corner sharpness.
- Structural layout logic derived from cartographic coordinate grids, structural wireframes, and geospatial indexing.
- Extreme discipline in color application: vast plains of pale stone and alabaster balanced with structural deep slate frameworks and hyper-focused emerald calibration vectors.
- Telemetric clarity, presenting complex satellite LIDAR analytics, biomass density metrics, and audited carbon yields without consumer-facing fluff.

## Colors

The palette enforces a clinical daylight baseline, pairing high-contrast structural slate with calibrated photosynthetic emerald accents.

- **Primary (`#0F172A`)**: Deep Slate. Forms the bedrock of typography, hairline structural borders, coordinate axes, and high-emphasis focal surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#10B981`)**: Emerald Signal. Reserved exclusively for confirmed telemetry, positive carbon sequestration trends, active sensor indices, and primary terminal execution triggers.
- **Tertiary (`#059669`)**: Viridian Metric. Deeper structural green used for metric charts, data point fills, active state halos, and telemetry toggles.
- **Neutral Surface (`#F8FAFC`)**: Alabaster White. Serves as the global backdrop, evoking architectural vellum or clinical laboratory paper.
- **Structural Sub-neutrals**:
  - Border Hairline: `#E2E8F0` (Base architectural subdivision lines)
  - Dark Structural Rule: `#0F172A` (Terminal borders and active panel boundaries)
  - Telemetry Muted: `#64748B` (Metadata, coordinate keys, labels)
  - Surface Elevation 1: `#FFFFFF` (Data cards, inspection panels)

## Typography

The type system is built on a tripartite hierarchy:

1. **Narrative Anchor (Epilogue)**: Used for high-impact landing headlines and architectural section entries. Its geometric, structural, and slightly unconventional letterforms deliver presence without decorative softness.
2. **Editorial & Analytical Clarity (Public Sans)**: Provides neutral, clear body copy and long-form analysis. It balances technical readability with institutional credibility.
3. **Telemetry & Instrumentation (JetBrains Mono)**: Applied across all data visualization axes, geospatial coordinate feeds, data tables, micro-tags, and interactive controls (CTA buttons, terminal toggles). All telemetry labels must use uppercase casing with generous tracking to emulate raw satellite readout interfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is disciplined by a **Strict 12-Column Architectural Matrix** overlaid with hairline visual coordinate guides:

- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12-column grid with a maximum container width of `1440px`, 24px gutters, and 32px side margins. Data readouts align to structural subdivisions (e.g., 3-column telemetry sidebar, 9-column spatial viewport).
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: 8-column grid with 16px gutters, 24px margins. Subpanels collapse into stacked horizontal instrument zones.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px)**: 4-column fluid matrix, 12px gutters, 16px margins. Telemetry indices convert to full-width horizontal ticker strips.

Spacing adheres to an explicit 4px/8px modular scale. Vertical page sections are separated by structural hairline dividers rather than arbitrary whitespace margins, echoing architectural plan blueprints.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids blurred shadows or skeuomorphic depths, favoring an **Architectural Layering & Hairline Boundary** model:

- **Elevation Tier 0 (Ground Plane)**: The pure Alabaster base (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Elevation Tier 1 (Inspection Surfaces & Data Panels)**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) framed with a 1px solid border (`#E2E8F0`). Zero drop-shadow.
- **Elevation Tier 2 (Active/Targeted Asset Nodes)**: Crisp, 1px deep slate (`#0F172A`) perimeter with a faint, non-blurred 2px offset border (`box-shadow: 2px 2px 0px 0px #0F172A`).
- **Overlays, Tooltips, & Context Terminals**: Deep Slate surfaces (`#0F172A`) with high-contrast text and a 1px emerald (`#10B981`) accent line on the leading edge.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary is strictly **Zero Radius (Sharp)**:

- Every boundary, container, button, tag, and modal features razor-sharp 90-degree corners (`border-radius: 0px`).
- Technical accents can employ 45-degree chamfer cuts (dog-eared corners) on focal status labels or primary action triggers to echo hardware avionics and surveying equipment.
- No rounded pills, circle avatars, or soft bubble containers exist in this system.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary CTA**: Deep Slate background (`#0F172A`), white text (`#FFFFFF`) in `JetBrains Mono` bold uppercase. 1px solid `#0F172A` border. Right chevron or coordinate glyph prefix. On hover, background shifts to `#10B981` with text snapping to `#0F172A`. 0px radius.
- **Secondary / Terminal Trigger**: Transparent background, 1px solid `#0F172A` frame, Deep Slate text. Hover triggers a solid `#0F172A` fill with white text.
- **Telemetry Action**: Outlined in `#E2E8F0`, with a persistent leading status square (3px by 3px in `#10B981`).

### Chips & Telemetry Tags
- Monospaced typography (`JetBrains Mono`, 11px).
- Structural construction: 1px solid outline in `#E2E8F0` or `#0F172A`, alabaster or white background, uppercase label accompanied by dynamic metric status indicator (e.g., `[SYS: VERIFIED]`, `[AOI: 41.40338, 2.17403]`).

### Cards & Asset Tiles
- Pure white container with 1px border (`#E2E8F0`).
- Header zone separated by a 1px horizontal rule, featuring asset index ID on the left and live monitoring status tag on the right.
- No drop shadows; hover states activate a 1px slate outer framing (`#0F172A`).

### Form Controls & Inputs
- Text inputs use transparent or white backgrounds with an underline or full 1px `#E2E8F0` enclosure that transitions to `#0F172A` on focus.
- Monospaced helper labels positioned directly above inputs in `#64748B`.
- Checkboxes and radios are strictly square (zero radius) with high-contrast solid slate checks or emerald indicator blocks.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Geospatial Coordinate Strip**: Fixed architectural HUD bar at section tops displaying dynamic latitude/longitude, LIDAR resolution scale (`0.25m/px`), and current timestamp.
- **Biomass Delta Inspector**: High-density comparative split panel with crosshair grid lines (`#E2E8F0`) and emerald telemetry data points.
