How Carbon Credits Actually Work

It’s a pretty straightforward exchange. Trees naturally pull carbon pollution out of the air as they grow. When you plant trees on empty or degraded land, you earn "carbon credits" based on how much carbon your new forest captures. Big companies that can't easily reduce their own pollution will buy these credits from you to balance things out. Basically, you get paid to restore the environment.
